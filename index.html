Arch Linux: The Breakdown

Arch Linux is not just an OS; it's a component set. To modify it, you must understand the four pillars:

  1. The Bootloader: GRUB or Systemd-boot. This is where your customization starts.
  2. The Kernel: The bridge between hardware and software.
  3. The Shell: Zsh, Fish, or Bash.
  4. The Display Server: Wayland (Recommended for 2026) or X11.

Building Your Own Distro

To turn Arch into your own branded "Distro," follow the Horizon Method:

# 1. Create a custom configuration script
# 2. Package your dotfiles into a .zst package
# 3. Use 'archiso' to build your custom ISO
sudo pacman -S archiso
cp -r /usr/share/archiso/configs/releng/ ./my-distro
# Modify packages.x86_64 to include your AUR helpers
./build.sh -v
